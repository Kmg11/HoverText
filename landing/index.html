Hover Text

Read any on-screen text, huge and clear

Hold Left Ctrl and point your pointer at any text on screen. A large, crisp copy appears right next to the cursor — sharp at any size, from any app.

Tooltips, button labels, menus, form fields — if the app exposes text, Hover Text can read it. Nothing is uploaded; it all stays on your machine.

Tired of squinting at your screen?

Tiny text, everywhere

Tooltips, button labels, menus, and dense paragraphs are easy to miss when they're small.

Zoom is a blurry mess

Screen magnifiers blow up the picture. You get a fuzzy close-up instead of a readable line.

Text you can't grab

Labels and menus often aren't selectable, so you can't copy them or read them larger.

How it works

Three seconds, zero effort

1

Hold the trigger

Hold Left Ctrl (or any key or combination you choose).

2

Point at text

Move your mouse over any text on screen.

3

Read it big

A large popup shows the text right at your cursor. Release to hide — or copy it on release.

Why it's different

Crisp because it's real text

Sharp at any size

Hover Text reads the actual text content, so it stays razor sharp no matter how large you make it.

Never gets in your way

The popup is always-on-top and click-through. It never steals focus and never blocks your click.

Works across apps

Buttons, menus, tooltips, form fields, paragraphs — if the app exposes text, Hover Text can read it.

Features

Everything you need, nothing you don't

  • Custom trigger key — Ctrl, Ctrl+Shift, anything you prefer
  • Copy hovered text to your clipboard on release
  • Dark and light themes
  • Anchored overlay or follow-the-cursor mode
  • Launch with Windows
  • 100% local and private — nothing ever leaves your machine
